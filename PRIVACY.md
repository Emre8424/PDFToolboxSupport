# PDF Toolbox privacy policy

PDF Toolbox processes scans, images, PDFs, handwritten signatures and recognized text on your iPhone or iPad. The app has no document server and no user account. It does not upload document content to the developer, Google or an OCR provider.

## Local documents

Camera access is used only to scan documents after you choose Scan. The Apple photo picker accesses only the images you select. Selected iCloud photos may need to download from Apple before processing. PDFs imported through Files are copied to the app's local Documents folder. Files are protected using iOS file protection and excluded from automatic backups. Deleting the app removes its local documents. Export important documents yourself.

The app keeps PDFs until you delete them or uninstall the app. Unsaved edits and signatures are held for the current workflow. Sharing exports create protected temporary files; these are removed on the next launch. Recognized text is processed locally and remains in the workflow until copied or exported. Clipboard contents follow iOS clipboard behavior.

## Sharing and printing

PDF Toolbox transfers a document only through your chosen action, such as a Share Sheet destination, Files export or AirPrint printer. The receiving app, cloud provider or printer applies its own privacy and retention rules. Documents exported outside PDF Toolbox are not deleted by removing the local original.

## Advertising

Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform provide a small banner in the document browser and regional privacy choices. They require an internet connection. Ads do not appear in the camera scanner or signature editor. All features work when advertisements or consent services are unavailable.

The app requests non-personalized ads, disables publisher first-party identifiers and does not request access to Apple's advertising identifier. Advertising services may still process approximate location derived from network information, device identifiers subject to iOS restrictions, ad interactions and diagnostic/performance information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Document names, pages, document text, OCR results and signatures are never supplied in ad requests.

Regional consent choices appear through Google's consent interface. Where required, you can reopen Advertising privacy choices in Settings. Google's policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.
